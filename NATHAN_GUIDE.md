## Adding a New Project

1. Click **Project** in the left sidebar
2. Click the **+** button at the top
3. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title:** The name of the project (e.g., "Grey Goose Launch Event")
   - **Vimeo URL:** Paste your Vimeo link (e.g., `https://vimeo.com/123456789`)
   - **Category:** Choose from the dropdown, or use **Custom Category** if your category isn't listed
   - **Featured on homepage:** Toggle ON if you want this on the homepage (maximum 3 allowed)
   - **Date:** The date of the project or shoot
   - **Description (optional):** A brief description of the project
4. Click **Publish** at the bottom

Your project will appear on the site within 1 minute.

---

## Editing an Existing Project

1. Click **Project** in the sidebar
2. Find and click the project you want to edit
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Publish** at the bottom

---

## Deleting a Project

1. Open the project you want to delete
2. Click the **three dots menu** (⋯) at the top right
3. Select **Delete**
4. Confirm deletion
5. **Important:** Publish the deletion (you'll see a deleted draft to publish)

---

## Content Limits

- **Homepage:** Maximum 3 featured projects allowed
- If you try to feature a 4th project, you'll see this error message:
  _"There are already 3 featured projects. Un-feature another project first."_
- To fix: Un-feature another project first, then feature the new one

---

## Managing Services ("What We Offer")

1. Click **Service** in the left sidebar
2. Click **+** to add a new service
3. Enter:
   - **Service:** The name of the service (e.g., "Event Videography")
   - **Display Order:** A number (lower numbers appear first)
4. Click **Publish**

Services appear in the "What We Offer" section on the homepage About area.

---

## Managing "Trusted By" Clients

1. Click **Trusted By** in the left sidebar
2. Click **+** to add a new client
3. Enter:
   - **Client / Brand Name:** The name (e.g., "Grey Goose")
   - **Display Order:** A number (lower numbers appear first)
4. Click **Publish**

These appear in the "Trusted By" section on the homepage About area.

---

## Updating Production Kit Description

1. Click **Production Kit** in the left sidebar
2. Edit the **Description** field
3. Click **Publish**

This text appears in the "Production Kit" section on the homepage About area.
